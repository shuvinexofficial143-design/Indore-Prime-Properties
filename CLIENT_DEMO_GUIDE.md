# Shuvi 360 client demonstration
Open /demo, browse fictional catalog, explore the Royal Emerald Villa with orbit/zoom, walk inside the procedural concept villa, try synthetic 360 panoramas, compare, estimate EMI and open brochure. The 3D is a conceptual visualization, not a survey or reconstruction of an actual home. Use conversational property search in Hindi or English.
With a database, open /demo/sandbox for an isolated two-hour practice workspace. It has sample listings and leads; edits and bookings never change the shared portfolio. No real account or paid provider is used in this sandbox.
Authorized owner: sign in, add/edit a fictional property, assign agent, create follow-up, use weekly appointment calendar, upload private media when storage configured, review before publishing, inspect aggregate reports and campaign links. Team and document vault have role-scoped access. For customer demo use only authorized fictional account/contact data, save properties, preferences and request/cancel fictional visit.
Studio: try local floor plan to 3D GLB and photo brightness/contrast/saturation without paid API. Optional Meshy/OpenAI adapters are not active. Automation is explicitly local simulation; no WhatsApp, Instagram or AI phone call is sent. Do not share admin credentials or submit real customer data. Public deployment not available yet.
